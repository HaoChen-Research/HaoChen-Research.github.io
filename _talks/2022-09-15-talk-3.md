Systematic study of WO₃₋ₓ thin films prepared by RF sputtering and annealing, revealing tunable structural, optical, dielectric, and electrical properties. Demonstrated strong potential as IR transparent conductors and explored integration into 1D photonic bandgap structures, showing electric field enhancement at the surface.
