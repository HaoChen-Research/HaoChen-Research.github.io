This work investigates WO<sub>3−x</sub> thin films prepared by RF sputtering and annealing, demonstrating tunable structural, optical, dielectric, and electrical properties. The results highlight their potential as IR transparent conductors and their integration into 1D photonic bandgap structures, enabling surface electric field enhancement.
