Controlled annealing environments modulate oxygen vacancy populations and local coordination in WO<sub>3-x</sub> thin films, driving changes in crystallization behavior and electronic structure. Synchrotron X-ray absorption reveals how defect chemistry governs microstructure and lifts W 5d orbital degeneracy.
