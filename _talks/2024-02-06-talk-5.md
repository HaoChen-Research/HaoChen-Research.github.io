Oxygen stoichiometry controls local ordering and electronic structure in WO₃₋ₓ: W 5d states split into two sub-bands with a dominant lower-energy component, tunable via annealing atmosphere. Changes in W–O coordination occur prior to long-range crystallization, indicating that electronic properties are governed by short-range order.
