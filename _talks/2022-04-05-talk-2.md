Investigated WO₃₋ₓ thin films as emerging transparent conductive materials. RF-sputtered films exhibit smooth morphology and n-type conductivity with extended near-IR transparency. Annealing tunes optoelectronic properties, with low-temperature treatment optimizing performance and demonstrating strong potential compared to conventional TCFs in the near-IR.
