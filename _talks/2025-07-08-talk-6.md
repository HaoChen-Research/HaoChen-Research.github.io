Quantitative non-negative matrix factorization (NMF) analysis of RIXS datasets resolves mixed-valence tungsten states (W⁴⁺, W⁵⁺, W⁶⁺) in WO<sub>3-x</sub>, enabling extraction of the previously obscured W⁵⁺ component. The results show that W⁴⁺-rich configurations dominate optical absorption and electrical conduction, with infrared response consistent with small-polaron hopping between mixed-valence sites in amorphous WO<sub>3-x</sub>.
